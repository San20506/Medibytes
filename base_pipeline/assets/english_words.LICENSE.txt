english_words.txt is the set of whole alphabetic words (3+ letters) in the
`bert-base-uncased` WordPiece vocabulary (the vocabulary file shipped with
sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2), published by Google Research under the
Apache License 2.0: https://github.com/google-research/bert

It is used only as a list of ordinary English words, so that
`context_repair` never offers one of them to the drug-name matcher.
